1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & WHAT THIS DATA IS
================================================================================
Silicon (Si) is a next-generation high-capacity anode material for lithium-ion 
batteries, offering a theoretical gravimetric capacity of ~3579 mAh/g (for Li15Si4)
to ~4200 mAh/g (for Li4.4Si), compared to 372 mAh/g for conventional graphite. 
However, silicon suffers from extreme volumetric expansion (~300%), severe 
voltage hysteresis, structural amorphization, and rapid capacity fade due to 
mechanical fracture and continuous Solid Electrolyte Interphase (SEI) growth.

Standard electrochemical continuum models (e.g., standard Pseudo-2D Doyle-Fuller-
Newman models) fail to capture the multi-phase transitions, path-dependent 
thermodynamics, and mechanical stress coupling inherent to silicon anodes.

THIS DATASET & FRAMEWORK REPRESENTS:
1. Atomistic Simulation Data: Calculated thermodynamic free energies, chemical 
   potentials, volume expansions, and diffusivities across varying Li-Si 
   stoichiometries (LixSi, 0 <= x <= 3.75) derived from Machine Learning Neural 
   Network Potentials (Matlantis® PFP) and Density Functional Theory (DFT).
2. Multi-Species Kinetic Formulation: A continuum mathematical framework that 
   models amorphous LixSi not as a single ideal solid solution, but as a system 
   of multiple discrete/intermediate LixSi species participating in concurrent 
   and consecutive charge-transfer reactions.
3. Stress-Coupled Continuum Model Parameters: COMSOL Multiphysics® v6.3 model 
   files and parameter sets incorporating Butler-Volmer kinetics, stress-dependent 
   overpotentials, volume changes, and concentration-dependent diffusivities.

2. HOW THE DATA WAS COLLECTED (METHODOLOGY & SOURCES)
================================================================================
The data generation follows a multi-scale workflow bridging atomistic physics 
and continuum electrochemistry:

A. Atomistic Scale Data Collection (DFT & Neural Network Potentials):
   - Software Platforms: AMS GUI 2025.1 (SCM) and Matlantis® (Preferred Networks).
   - Atomic Potential: Preferred Potential (PFP), a universal deep neural 
     network force field capable of accurately modeling Li-Si alloys across 
     amorphous and crystalline phases.
   - Optimization Algorithms: FIRE / FIRE2 (Fast Inertial Relaxation Engine) for 
     structural relaxation of amorphous LixSi structures.
   - Outputs Generated:
     * Formation energy ΔG_f(x) as a function of Li concentration x.
     * Open Circuit Potential (OCP) vs. Li/Li+ derived via Nernstian integration 
       of chemical potential μ_Li(x) = (∂G/∂N_Li).
     * Partial molar volumes V_bar(x) and density changes.
     * Activation barriers for Li self-diffusion D_Li(x).

B. Macro/Continuum Model Calibration Data:
   - Platform: COMSOL Multiphysics® v6.3 with the Battery Design Module®.
   - Governing Equations:
     * Multi-species mass transport (Fickian diffusion with chemical potential 
       gradients and stress-driven flux).
     * Charge conservation in liquid electrolyte and solid electrode phases.
     * Butler-Volmer multi-reaction charge transfer kinetics for reaction 
       pathways: Li+ + e- + Sp_i <-> Sp_j.
     * Solid mechanics (elastoplastic deformation and strain-induced stress).

C. Experimental Validation Data:
   - Galvanostatic Charge/Discharge (GCD) profiles at C-rates (C/20 to 1C).
   - Galvanostatic Intermittent Titration Technique (GITT) for equilibrium OCP 
     and apparent diffusion coefficients.
   - Cyclic Voltammetry (CV) peak analysis for identifying oxidation/reduction 
     reaction thresholds.

4. STEP-BY-STEP DATA PROCESSING PIPELINE
================================================================================

STEP 1: Atomistic Parameter Extraction
  1. Generate random/amorphous LixSi supercells (x = 0.0 to 3.75).
  2. Relax cell vectors and atomic positions using Matlantis PFP / AMS GUI 2025.1.
  3. Calculate equilibrium total energy E(x) and hydrostatic pressure P.
  4. Compute Open Circuit Voltage curve: E_OCV(x) = -[G(LixSi) - G(Si) - x*G(Li)] / (x*F).

STEP 2: Multi-Species Discretization & Kinetic Formulation
  1. Divide continuous stoichiometry x into M discrete representative species 
     (e.g., Si_amorphous, Li0.4Si, Li1.1Si, Li2.2Si, Li3.5Si, Li15Si4).
  2. Formulate reaction rate equations r_k for transition k between species i and j:
     r_k = k_k_f * c_i * exp(alpha_f * F * eta_k / RT) - k_k_r * c_j * exp(-alpha_r * F * eta_k / RT)
  3. Include stress coupling: Overpotential eta_k is shifted by mechanical strain energy 
     Omega * sigma_hydrostatic.

STEP 3: Finite Element Continuum Solution (COMSOL 6.3)
  1. Mesh spherical/structured Si anode particle geometry.
  2. Define boundary conditions: Li+ flux at particle surface set by electrolyte 
     current density; zero flux at particle core center.
  3. Solve coupled system of non-linear PDEs (Mass transport + Mechanics + Charge balance).

STEP 4: Output Synthesis & Validation
  1. Export voltage vs. state-of-charge (SOC) curves for lithiation and delithiation.
  2. Compare voltage hysteresis envelope against experimental GITT data.
  3. Map radial stress profiles (radial stress σ_r and tangential stress σ_θ) to 
     predict crack initiation thresholds.
